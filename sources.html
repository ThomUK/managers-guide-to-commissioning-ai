Sources and how prices are shown

Every price, power and performance figure in this course comes from one of the sources below. All were checked on 13 November 2026. Prices and benchmarks date quickly, so treat every figure as a snapshot.

This course was drafted with AI assistance. Figures were checked on the date above.

VAT

All UK prices in this course are shown including VAT at 20%, because most public bodies and healthcare providers cannot reclaim VAT on hardware or off-the-shelf software, so the VAT-inclusive price is the real cost to them; organisations able to reclaim VAT will pay one-sixth less.

Where a source gave an ex-VAT price, or a US dollar list price with no tax, 20% has been added. Where the seller’s VAT basis was not stated, it has been assumed to be ex VAT and 20% added; these are marked “assumed ex VAT at source” next to the figure.

Currency conversion

Figures marked “converted from USD” are a US dollar price × 0.74 × 1.2, rounded. The exchange rate used is $1 = £0.74; the interbank rate was about £0.743 on 6 to 9 November 2026 (S20). On pages with prices, select any price to see the original figure and its basis.

Electricity

Yearly electricity costs use 30p per kWh including VAT as a round illustration. The UK average business rate was 24.14p per kWh ex VAT in Q1 2026 government data, about 29p with VAT; November 2026 quotes were higher (S22, S23).

How far to trust these sources

Several of these sources are trade blogs and reseller pages, not primary sources. Before relying on a price for a real decision, check it against the vendor or retailer page directly.
